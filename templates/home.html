Questions

What volunteers and coordinators ask before they start.

Browse open opportunities

Who can post an opportunity?

A coordinator whose organization an administrator has approved. Until then the organization stays pending.

When do my hours count?

Once the coordinator of that opportunity verifies your log. Pending and rejected logs add nothing.

When do I get a certificate?

At 25, 50 and 100 verified hours. Each one is a PDF with its own certificate ID.

How does someone check my certificate?

Every certificate has a public page at its ID, showing your name, the hours and the date it was issued.

What happens when an event is full?

The coordinator cannot confirm more volunteers than the places the opportunity lists.

Can coordinators apply to their own events?

No. A coordinator cannot apply to an opportunity that their own organization runs.
